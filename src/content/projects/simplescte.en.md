## The context

Freight is an industry where downtime is a direct loss. Every minute a driver spends
fighting a form is a minute the truck isn't moving — and that completely shapes the
product's design priorities.

## My role

I worked on the platform's front-end, focused on the issuance flow and the mobile
experience. It's the project that taught me the most about the real cost of interface
friction: here, one less step in the form isn't a UX detail, it's revenue for the client.
